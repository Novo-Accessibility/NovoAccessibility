Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

Who we are

Novo Accessibility is a service of Novo Industries Inc., an Alberta corporation (BN 716914023) based in Calgary, Alberta, Canada.

What information we collect

When you book a call through our scheduling tool (Calendly), we collect the information you provide: your name, email address, phone number, and any details you include in the booking form. When you download documents from our website, no personal information is collected.

How we collect it

Call booking is processed through Calendly, a third-party scheduling service. Calendly transmits your booking information to us via email notification. We do not use cookies for tracking or analytics on this website.

How we use your information

We use the information you provide to respond to your inquiry, schedule and conduct introductory calls, prepare proposals, and communicate about our services.

Who we share it with

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. Information may be shared with service providers who assist in delivering our services (such as scheduling and email platforms), but only to the extent necessary to fulfill your request.

How long we keep it

We retain your information for as long as necessary to provide our services and comply with legal obligations. You may request deletion of your information at any time.

Your rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal information. To exercise these rights, contact us at carl@novoaccessibility.com.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The updated version will be posted on this page with a revised date.
