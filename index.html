How the Pilot Works

From field assessment to council-ready roadmap

A structured engagement that produces defensible, budget-ready accessibility data for municipal capital planning.

Assess

A qualified RHFAC Professional conducts structured on-site accessibility assessments and documents conditions across each selected facility using standardized data capture tools, photo documentation, and precision measurement.

Analyze and Prioritize

Findings are normalized across facilities and evaluated by risk, user impact, and relative cost. The result is a clear, defensible prioritization that supports sequencing decisions during budget review.

Plan

Results are translated into a council-ready capital roadmap aligned to municipal budget cycles. Quick wins, phased capital investments, and procurement language are included to support immediate action.

Why a Standalone Assessment

Addressing the most common internal question before it gets asked

Municipal teams frequently ask: "Why not have our engineer or internal team handle this?" The answer is structural.

Engineers optimize solutions, not portfolios

Engineering engagements are scoped to a specific project or problem. Accessibility planning requires a cross-facility view, ranking buildings against each other rather than solving each one independently.

Internal teams lack normalization tools

Facility staff understand individual buildings but rarely have the structured methodology to assess multiple buildings simultaneously against a consistent scoring framework.

Accessibility is cross-facility, not project-specific

A service counter height problem and a missing power door at a different facility compete for the same capital budget. Defensible prioritization requires comparing them on the same scale before design work begins.

This creates the missing sequencing layer

Most municipalities jump from policy commitments to renovation projects, skipping the portfolio-level evidence layer. This pilot fills that gap, producing the data that makes Year 1 capital submissions clear and justified.
