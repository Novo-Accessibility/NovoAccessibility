Methodology

Next Dollar Impact.

Most accessibility reports tell you what is wrong. Next Dollar Impact tells you what to fix first, ranked by how many compliance points each remediation delivers per dollar spent.

This matters when budgets are constrained. The first $25,000 you spend should close the most certification gap, not the most work and not the most convenient items.

“The purpose of the report is not documentation. It is a decision-support tool. Every section should make the next capital decision easier.”

FAQ

Common questions, direct answers.

What areas do you serve?

Novo Accessibility serves organizations across all of Canada.

How is the assessment priced?

Pricing depends on facility size, number of buildings, and location. Contact us for a scoping conversation and a written fixed-fee quote. No change orders on fixed-fee work.

Can this report be used for a grant application?

Yes. The scored findings, phased capital plan, and certification pathway table are built with grant writers in mind, including CMHC and Enabling Accessibility Fund submissions.

What does PSIB-eligible mean for our procurement process?

Federal departments with an Indigenous procurement target can award contracts through the Procurement Strategy for Indigenous Business without competitive tender. Novo counts toward that target.

How long does a pilot take?

A pilot covers 3 to 5 facilities over 6 to 8 weeks. The full report is delivered within 7 to 10 business days of the final site visit.
